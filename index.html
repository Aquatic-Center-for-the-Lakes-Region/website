Our vision: an aquatic, fitness, and recreational venue that fosters a healthy, safe, and connected Lakes Region.

Why we're here

The Lakes Region lost its community pool to a fire. We're a group of local residents working to replace it with a new aquatic center where everyone in the region can learn to swim, stay active, and spend time together.

We're open to a site anywhere in New Hampshire's Lakes Region.

Our mission

Providing swimming, water safety education, fitness programs, recreational opportunities, and lifelong learning experiences, creating a welcoming environment where individuals and families of all ages and abilities can grow, thrive, and build meaningful connections.

Where things stand

  • Incorporated as a nonprofit corporation
  • Application for 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status in progress
  • Building a team of volunteers, partners, and supporters
